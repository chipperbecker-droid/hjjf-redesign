Practice areas

Legal services for Delaware clients.

Browse the firm's services for individuals, families, businesses, property owners, associations, creditors, and governmental entities.

  1. A Alcoholic Beverage Licensing Licensing counsel for Delaware restaurants, retailers, manufacturers, distributors, taverns, taprooms, and hospitality businesses.
  2. B Business Law Delaware business law counsel for contracts, commercial transactions, business transfers, confidentiality matters, and strategic planning for local companies.
  3. C Civil Litigation Delaware litigation counsel for contract disputes, property claims, business torts, construction matters, personal injury claims, and court proceedings.
  4. Condominium and Homeowners Association Law Counsel for Delaware condominium and homeowners associations on assessments, governing documents, board governance, DUCIOA, and community disputes.
  5. Corporations & LLCs Delaware entity formation, operating agreements, corporate maintenance, registered agent support, and compliance for LLCs and corporations.
  6. D Domestic Law Delaware family law representation for divorce, custody, support, visitation, property division, and protection-from-abuse matters.
  7. L Landlord/Tenant Law Delaware landlord-tenant counsel for leases, possession actions, rent disputes, security deposits, habitability, and housing compliance.
  8. M Municipal Law Municipal and local government law counsel for Delaware towns, cities, counties, boards, agencies, and public bodies.
  9. P Personal Injury Personal injury representation for Delaware vehicle accidents, unsafe premises, serious injuries, insurance claims, and negligence matters.
  10. R Real Estate Delaware real estate counsel for closings, title review, refinancing, foreclosure, property disputes, and residential or commercial transactions.
  11. Registered Agent / Service of Process Delaware registered agent and service of process support for entities, businesses, and counsel needing a Delaware point of contact.
  12. T Trusts, Wills & Estates Delaware estate planning and probate guidance for wills, trusts, powers of attorney, living wills, estate administration, and incapacity planning.
  13. Z Zoning & Subdivision Delaware zoning and land-use counsel for subdivisions, variances, conditional uses, development approvals, ordinances, and appeals.
