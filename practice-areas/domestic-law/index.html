Domestic Law

Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher represents clients in Delaware family law matters that often arise during difficult and emotionally challenging periods. The firm provides guidance and advocacy in divorce, child custody disputes, child support, property division, and protection-from-abuse proceedings.

The firm assists with contested and uncontested divorce, alimony and spousal support, child custody and visitation, child support, equitable distribution of marital property, and high-asset divorce matters. This may include parenting plans, legal and physical custody issues, support calculations, retirement accounts, real estate, business interests, and other marital property concerns.

In matters involving domestic violence or threats of harm, the firm provides representation for individuals seeking protection-from-abuse orders and for individuals defending against false or unsupported allegations. The firm's attorneys work to pursue practical and sustainable outcomes while protecting the client's rights and family interests.

Delaware Family Law FAQ

Q.What family law matters does the firm handle?

The firm assists with divorce, custody, visitation, child support, alimony, property division, and protection-from-abuse matters in Delaware.

Q.What is equitable distribution in a Delaware divorce?

Equitable distribution is the process of dividing marital property and debt in a divorce. The result depends on the facts and does not always mean an equal division.

Q.Can custody and support issues be modified?

Some custody, visitation, or support orders may be modified when legal standards are met and circumstances justify a change. The facts and timing matter.
