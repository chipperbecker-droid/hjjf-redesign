Careers

We are hiring attorneys for our Dover and Rehoboth Beach offices.

Open positions (2)

Both positions are full-time and based in the office, with competitive salary and benefits.

Notice No. 1

Rehoboth Beach office · Full-time · In office

Associate Attorney, Real Estate

Our Rehoboth Beach office is looking for an associate with two to five years of real estate experience. The work includes residential and commercial settlements, title review, and appearances before Sussex County and town planning and zoning boards.

Requirements

  • J.D. and admission to the Delaware Bar (or sitting for the next exam)
  • 2–5 years of real estate or transactional experience
  • Strong drafting skills and attention to detail
Apply for the Rehoboth Beach position

Notice No. 2

Dover office · Full-time · In office

Associate Attorney, Litigation

Our Dover office is looking for an associate with at least three years of litigation experience. The work includes civil cases in Delaware's trial courts and advising the towns and public bodies we represent in Kent County.

Requirements

  • J.D. and admission to the Delaware Bar
  • 3+ years of litigation experience
  • Strong writing and courtroom skills
Apply for the Dover position
