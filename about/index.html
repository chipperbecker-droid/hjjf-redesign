No. 01

History

Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher, LLC is one of Southern Delaware’s oldest and largest law firms. The firm was founded on December 31, 1956, when David P. Buckson, later Attorney General and Lt. Governor of Delaware and a Family Court Judge, and George R. Wright, later Deputy Attorney General and Delaware Superior Court Judge, shared offices at 225 South State Street in Dover. William S. Hudson joined them around 1960, and the firm later became Hudson, Jones and Jaywork in 1977.

Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher is a full-service Delaware law firm serving individuals, families, businesses, property owners, associations, and public-sector clients from offices in Dover and Rehoboth Beach.

The firm provides legal counsel and representation across automobile accidents and other personal injury matters, civil litigation, business law, residential and commercial real estate transactions, municipal and zoning law, domestic relations, creditor relations, wills and estates, and administrative law.
