No. 04

Seven decades on South State Street.

  1. 1956

    December 31

    The firm is founded when David P. Buckson, later Attorney General and Lt. Governor of Delaware and a Family Court Judge, and George R. Wright, later Deputy Attorney General and Delaware Superior Court Judge, share offices at 225 South State Street in Dover.

  2. c. 1960

    William S. Hudson joins them.

  3. 1977

    The firm becomes Hudson, Jones and Jaywork.

  4. Today

    Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher, LLC is one of Southern Delaware’s oldest and largest law firms, with offices in Dover and Rehoboth Beach.
